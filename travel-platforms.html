The Underwriter

The Underwriter is paid every pass fee and funds every rescue card. You never fund anything.

The Underwriter issues the Flight Delay Pass under its licence. From the pass fees it is paid, it pays for a pre-loaded rescue card for every pass holder whose flight is delayed 3+ hours or cancelled, pays your share, and pays GetThere a platform fee to run the service.

Is paid every pass fee

The traveller pays GetThere, the Underwriter's authorized collection agent, through Stripe. For passes sold in your own checkout, you pay GetThere on one monthly statement. GetThere passes the fee to the Underwriter.

Funds every rescue card

Every pass holder whose flight is delayed 3+ hours or cancelled gets a pre-loaded rescue card, issued by a card provider the Underwriter chooses.

Pays your share

Your share of each pass fee is agreed with the Underwriter and paid monthly from the fees it is paid.

Risk, legal and privacy

Answers for your risk, legal and privacy teams

Regulatory position

The Flight Delay Pass is a policy, issued and underwritten by the Underwriter under its own licence. GetThere is its platform provider and authorized collection agent. The rescue card buys new flights in your booking tool only and is never paid out as cash.

You never hold the pass money

Every pass is paid to GetThere's Stripe account as the Underwriter's agent and passed on to the Underwriter. Your monthly share is worked out from those records, with no manual invoicing.

No payment card data

Payment card details are typed into Stripe's payment page. GetThere never sees them, and neither do you.

Only what the checkout needs

The traveller details you pass fill in the checkout. Date of birth and address are deleted once sales close, 48 hours before departure.
