Risk, legal and privacy

Answers for your risk, legal and privacy teams

A policy from the Underwriter

The Underwriter issues the pass under its own licence. The rescue card buys flights only and is never paid out as cash.

You never fund anything

Every pass fee is paid into the Underwriter's own account. The Underwriter holds the money, funds every rescue card and pays your share monthly.

No payment card data

Card details go into the Underwriter's payment gateway. Neither GetThere nor you see them.

No employee profiles

The holder's details are kept with each pass, never in a profile. Date of birth and address are deleted once sales close, 48 hours before departure.
