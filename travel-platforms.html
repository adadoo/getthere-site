Risk, legal and privacy

Answers for risk, legal and privacy teams

A policy from the Underwriter

Issued under the Underwriter's licence. The rescue card buys flights only, never cash.

You never fund anything

Pass fees go to the Underwriter, which funds rescue cards and pays your share monthly.

No payment card data

Card details go to the Underwriter's payment gateway. Neither you nor GetThere sees them.

No employee profiles

Holder details stay with each pass, not in a profile. Date of birth and address are deleted when sales close, 48 hours before departure.
