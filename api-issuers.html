For card issuers

API guide for card issuers

Version 1 · Draft · Written for the developers who build it. Keys, error format, retries, test mode and webhook signatures are on the basics page. Travel site? See the travel site guide. Loyalty programs build the same calls, plus crediting miles.

The short version. You list the Flight Delay Pass as one of your points redemption partners. Your own GetThere redemption page shows the cardholder where to forward their booking email, and lets them swap points for a voucher. That's two calls: GET /offer when the page opens, and POST /vouchers if they redeem. GetThere builds the rest: pricing, checkout with the voucher already applied, flight tracking, pre-loaded rescue cards, every message to the cardholder, and support.

1. Who builds what

You build

  • The "Just booked a flight?" push notification, from your own card data, after an airline charge. It opens your redemption page.
  • Your GetThere redemption page, in your app, beside your other redemption partners: it shows the forwarding address and takes the points if the cardholder redeems.
  • A webhook endpoint, to hear when a voucher is used or ends unused, so you can give the points back.
  • A push endpoint, push receipts and a purchase-match endpoint optional.

GetThere provides

  • Your forwarding address, quote@<issuer>.getthere.now, where cardholders send their booking email.
  • The partner API at https://api.getthere.now/partner/v1.
  • Checkout on https://<issuer>.getthere.now, in your colours, with the voucher already applied.
  • Every message to the cardholder: the price, the pass, and the rescue card on WhatsApp and by email.

2. The journey, screen by screen

One cardholder, Mia, pays an airline A$1,550 with your card and redeems 5,000 points for A$50 off her pass.

  1. Your app→Mia

    You send "Just booked a flight?"

    From your own card data, after an airline charge. Nothing reaches GetThere: card data stays with you.

  2. Your server→GetThere

    Your redemption page opens: you ask for an offer and show her address

    Show forward_to with a Copy button, following the address rules, and the option to redeem points.

    GET /partner/v1/offer?residence_country=AU&opened=true&customer_ref=C-88213 · details

  3. Mia→Your systems→GetThere

    If she redeems: you take the points and post the voucher

    Then change the page's copy: "Your A$50 voucher will be applied at checkout." The address stays the same.

    POST /partner/v1/vouchers with the offer's request_id · details

  4. Mia→GetThere

    She forwards her booking email

    To the address she copied, with or without a voucher. We read every flight and traveller on it.

  5. GetThere→Mia

    She gets a price and buys the pass

    We email her the plans within minutes. Checkout opens with the voucher applied, and her card pays the rest. Without a voucher, she pays by card.

  6. GetThere→Your webhook

    The voucher is used, or you give the points back

    voucher.used when her pass is bought. voucher.expired if she never forwards a booking or never buys: give her points back. Webhooks

3. GET /partner/v1/offer

You call GetThere · from your server, each time your redemption page opens

Makes a fresh offer: a code, carried in the offer's links and in the forwarding address, that ties a booking forwarded from it back to this visit to your page. You'll see how many cardholders opened it, forwarded a booking, got a price and bought a pass. Tell us the country your cardholder lives in: the booking they forward is then quoted by the underwriter selling there, with that country's tax already in each price, so the price in their email is the price at checkout.

GET /partner/v1/offer?residence_country=AU&opened=true&utm_source=app&utm_medium=push&utm_campaign=just-booked&customer_ref=C-88213
200 {"status": "offered",
     "residence_country": "AU",
     "customer_ref": "C-88213",
     "request_id": "k7f2q9xm",
     "forward_to": "quote+k7f2q9xm@<issuer>.getthere.now",
     "product_name": "Flight Delay Pass",
     "reference": "R-K7F2Q9XM"}
ParameterWhat it isWhy it matters
residence_country requiredThe 2-letter ISO country your cardholder lives in, like SG: the country on their card account.Picks the underwriter and the tax, so their quote email shows the full price. With a live key, a country we don't sell in yet answers {"status": "declined", "reason": "not_on_sale"}: don't offer the redemption.
utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign optional Where you show the offer. Up to 64 letters, digits, ., - or _, lowercased. src is short for utm_source.Your offer report splits by them, so you see which pushes, banners and campaigns sell passes.
referrer_url optionalOn a web page, the address the visitor came from.Counted by site.

customer_ref optionalYour own id for the customer: 1 to 128 printable characters, no spaces. Never a card number. Echoed in the answer.It comes back on every webhook event, push and purchase check about this customer, even for a later booking they forward without an offer, so you find them by your own id rather than by email. A loyalty program uses it to credit miles.
FieldWhat it isWhy it matters
statusoffered, or declined with reason not_on_sale where we don't sell yet.Offer the redemption only when it's offered.
request_idThis offer's code, the one in its forwarding address. It isn't a quote: quotes come once the cardholder forwards a booking, and are traced back to it.Post the voucher with it, and keep it with the redemption in your points system.
forward_toThe cardholder's forwarding address for this offer, quote+<code>@<issuer>.getthere.now. While your partner is in test mode, that address makes test passes. Once you're live, a test key gets test+<code>@<issuer>.getthere.now instead, so test bookings still make test passes.Only this full address carries the code. If you show it on your own screen, follow the rules below.
product_nameThe product's name, to show.Use it as is.
referenceWhat our pages show the cardholder as "Your reference".Quote it to us and we can find the cardholder's journey.

Errors: 401, 403 and 503 while you're paused (see errors). If the call fails, don't take any points: show an error and let them try again.

4. POST /partner/v1/vouchers

You call GetThere · from your server, if the cardholder redeems, once you've taken the points

A pass voucher for the offer you just made. It works only on the booking forwarded to that offer's forward_to, and its checkout opens with it applied: the cardholder never types a code.

POST /partner/v1/vouchers
{"kind": "points",
 "request_id": "k7f2q9xm",
 "amount": 50, "currency": "AUD",
 "note": "5,000 points"}
FieldWhat it isWhy it matters
request_id requiredThe offer's, from GET /offer. Ties the voucher to her booking. It takes the offer's customer_ref and ends with it.
amount, currency requiredWhat the voucher pays, tax included, in your own currency if you like. If the pass is priced in another, checkout converts it at the day's rate and shows both.The pass price is only known once her booking is forwarded: about 7.5% of her fare. A fixed amount costs you exactly what you priced in points, and her card pays the rest.
kindpoints.Shown on your monthly statement.
note optionalUp to 200 characters, such as the points taken.Comes back on the voucher and its webhooks.

The answer is the voucher, with its code and status issued. Every voucher field, and how to look one up or cancel it, is on the basics page.

WebhookWhat happenedWhat to do with the points
voucher.usedHer pass was bought with it.Nothing: they're spent.
voucher.expiredThe offer ended and the voucher was never used.Give them back.

Each month we invoice you only for the vouchers used, in the currency your contract with us is in, at the rate on the day of each checkout. Without a contract currency, it's each pass's currency (a voucher in another currency at the rate its checkout used). If a pass is refunded, the card part goes back and the voucher stays used, so don't return the points.

5. Showing the forwarding address

Your redemption page shows forward_to with a Copy button as soon as it opens, whether or not the cardholder redeems. Once they do, add: "Your voucher will be applied at checkout." The cardholder copies the address, switches to their email, and forwards the airline's confirmation.

The address shown can't be copied on its own. Only the full forward_to carries the offer's code, which is what finds the voucher. So:
  • show the address without its +code: quote@<issuer>.getthere.now, easier to read;
  • make it unselectable, with no long-press menu and no automatic mail link (on the web: user-select: none; -webkit-touch-callout: none and <meta name="format-detection" content="email=no">);
  • copy the whole forward_to when the address or the Copy button is tapped.
A booking forwarded to the plain address still gets a price, but not the voucher.

Don't open a mail app with the address pre-filled. A cardholder can't forward an existing email from a new message, so it only gets in the way.

6. Your push endpoint optional

GetThere calls you · when a pass is bought, a rescue card is ready, and a rescue card is used

Without it, we send no pushes, and the cardholder still gets everything on WhatsApp and by email. With it, your app alerts them too. Pushes are alerts only: the rescue card itself always goes on WhatsApp and by email.

POST <your push URL>
Authorization: Bearer <the token you gave us>
Idempotency-Key: 5b1f…

{"push_id": "5b1f…",
 "customer": {"email": "mia@example.com", "customer_ref": "C-88213"},
 "type": "card_issued",
 "title": "Your flight is 3h 50m late. Your pre-loaded rescue card with A$2,325 is ready.",
 "body": "We've sent it to you on WhatsApp and by email.",
 "link": null}
FieldWhat it isWhy it matters
push_idUnique per push, and also the Idempotency-Key header.We may retry. Show a push id only once, and report on it in push receipts.
customer.emailThe email the cardholder forwarded their booking from.How you find the cardholder. If you can't match it, answer 202 and report not_delivered.
typepass_purchased, card_issued or card_used.card_issued is time-critical: the cardholder may be at the airport. Send it at the highest priority your app has.
title, bodyReady to show, in the wording agreed with you.Show them unchanged. The wording is agreed with the Underwriter.
linkA page to open, or null.Where tapping the push should go.
202 {"accepted": true, "reference": "<your id for the push>"}

Answer as soon as you've accepted it. Report delivery later with push receipts. Give us a separate test URL and token if you have a test system. Otherwise test pushes are only recorded.

7. POST /partner/v1/push-receipts optional

You call GetThere · in batches, whenever you have them

{"receipts": [
  {"push_id": "5b1f…", "status": "delivered", "at": "2026-11-20T07:41:05+00:00"},
  {"push_id": "9c2e…", "status": "opted_out"}
]}
FieldWhat it isWhy it matters
push_id requiredFrom the push we sent.Ties the receipt to the push.
status requireddelivered, not_delivered or opted_out.opted_out stops every further push to that cardholder, so you honour their choice. not_delivered tells us WhatsApp and email are the only way to reach them.
at optionalWhen, ISO with a time zone (Z or an offset). Defaults to when we receive it. A time we can't read gives unknown_push.Shows how quickly the rescue card alert reached them.
200 {"results": [{"push_id": "5b1f…", "result": "ok"}, {"push_id": "9c2e…", "result": "ok"}]}

Each result is ok, bad_status or unknown_push (not yours, or the other mode). A body without a receipts list gets 422.

8. Your purchase-match endpoint optional

GetThere calls you · when a cardholder forwards a booking that shows its airfare

A yes confirms the booking is real and its fare is right, so we can offer a pass even when the fare looks unusually high for the route. You only answer yes or no: no transaction data comes back to us.

POST <your API>/v1/getthere/purchase-match
Authorization: Bearer <the token you gave us>

{"email": "mia@example.com", "customer_ref": "C-88213", "airline": "QF", "seller": null, "amount": "1550.00",
 "currency": "AUD", "booked_after": "2025-11-20T07:41:05.120345+00:00"}
200 {"match": true}
FieldWhat it isWhy it matters
emailThe email the booking was forwarded from.How you find the cardholder. Not found is false.
customer_refYour own id for the customer, when you gave one on GET /offer; otherwise null.Find the cardholder by it first, then by email.
airlineThe airline whose flights were bought, 2-letter code.Match on the merchant when seller is null.
sellerWho charged the card when it wasn't the airline, such as a travel agency ("Trip.com"), or null.Then the charge to look for is from the seller, not the airline.
amount, currencyThe fare on the booking.Answer true within 1% of it, so currency rounding doesn't cause a no.
booked_afterThe earliest charge to look at: a year before we ask.Keeps the search to recent charges.

We wait up to 5 seconds. A timeout or error never blocks the cardholder: the booking goes through our other checks.

9. Webhooks

Give us a test and a live address. You need it for voucher.used and voucher.expired, to know when to give points back. We also tell your systems about every pass your cardholders buy: issued, ended, an email that bounced, a rescue card issued and used. The format, retries, every event and how to check the signature are on the basics page. They're most useful for your customer service: when a cardholder calls, your agent sees what we sent and when.

10. Crediting miles loyalty programs

Members earn your miles on the passes they buy. What you give them comes out of your share of the pass fee, as you agree with the Underwriter, the way a merchant commission does. GetThere tells you who bought and what they paid; you credit the miles in your own system.

  1. Name the member on every offer. Call GET /offer with customer_ref set to your own id for the member. It comes back on every event about them, even for a later booking they forward without an offer.
  2. Credit pending miles on pass.issued. Find the member by data.customer_ref and earn on data.fee_before_tax, never on amount_paid, which includes sales tax. Store the policy_id with the credit.
  3. Take them back if the pass is refunded. On pass.ended with reason: refunded, reverse the pending miles for that policy_id.
  4. Confirm them once the trip starts. Make the miles final after the first flight's departure date, if no refund came. A pass that ends with reason: expired (no delay) or sends a rescue card keeps its miles.
EventWhat to do with the miles
pass.issuedCredit as pending: fee_before_tax × your earn rate, to customer_ref.
pass.ended, reason: refunded or flight_cancelled_before_watchReverse them.
pass.ended, reason: replacedReverse them. The new pass sends its own pass.issued, which you credit as usual.
pass.ended, reason: expired; rescue_card.issuedKeep them.
No customer_ref on the eventThe member never came through one of your offers with a reference. Match on the email in your push, or leave it.

Check what you credited against your monthly report of passes sold.

12. Go-live checklist

  • Your push wording, and our push wording if you use your push endpoint, is agreed with us and the Underwriter.
  • The test cases above pass.
  • Your redemption page shows the address following the rules, and gives points back on voucher.expired.
  • Your live push URL, purchase-match URL and token are with us, if you use them.
  • Your live webhook address is set and checks signatures, if you use one.
  • We issue your live key and switch you live. Swap the key: the code stays the same.
