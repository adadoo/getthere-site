Privacy notice

How we use your data

For every pass, wherever you bought it · Effective 16 October 2026 · Draft for legal review
Your terms are linked from every email we send you.

The short version. We use the booking you forward to price your pass, track your flights and send your pre-loaded rescue card. We don't sell your data and we don't send you marketing. We keep no profile of you: your GetThere account is only your email address. Everything else is kept with the booking or pass you gave it for, and deleted with it (section 7).

1. Who we are

The Flight Delay Pass is sold by the company your pass's terms name as selling it and funding your pre-loaded rescue cards. On a partner's page, that is the Underwriter, such as an insurer, and the pass is a policy it issues. A page that is still in testing says it is a consumer demo: passes bought there are test passes and no real money moves. GetThere ("we", "us") runs the service for that company, including when it's offered under a partner's name, and is responsible for the personal data described here. The selling company is also responsible for the data it receives (section 4). Contact us at privacy@getthere.now, or at the help address in any email we've sent you.

2. What we collect

  • The booking email you forward, including its attachments: the names of the travellers, the booking reference, flights, fare and taxes, and anything else the airline put in it, such as contact details or the last digits of the card used.
  • Your email address, the one you forward from. It is all your GetThere account holds: we don't keep a profile of you across bookings.
  • Your mobile number, which you give at checkout so we can send your pre-loaded rescue card there. We send a code to it on WhatsApp to check it's yours, and we only send you WhatsApp messages once you've entered that code. It is kept with that pass, not on your account. If you bought through a partner's own checkout, it comes from the confirmation email instead.
  • Your legal name, date of birth, home address and the country you live in, which you give at checkout so the pre-loaded rescue card can be issued in your name. They are kept with that pass, not on your account, so you give them again for each pass. If you leave checkout without buying, we delete them once that price can no longer be bought.
  • Payment details: you type your card details into the payment form of the selling company's payment provider, embedded on our checkout page. We never see or store your card number; we get only the payment's result and amount.
  • Your pre-loaded rescue card's use: where it was used, the amount and when.
  • Flight status for your flights, from flight-data providers.
  • When you open an offer page, such as one linked from a partner's app or an ad: the page you came from, any campaign tags in the link you followed (where the link was shared and which campaign it belongs to, such as utm_source), and when. A booking you forward from that page is linked to the visit.
  • Delivery records for the messages we send you, and basic technical logs.

3. Why we use it

  • To price your pass and decide whether we can offer one, including checks that the booking and fare are real.
  • To track your flights and send your pre-loaded rescue card when a flight is delayed or cancelled.
  • To send you your price, pass, alerts and pre-loaded rescue card, and to answer your questions.
  • To count how many people see an offer and go on to forward a booking, which links and sites bring them there, and to tell real visits apart from automated ones (bots and link previews).
  • To prevent fraud and misuse, and to meet legal and accounting obligations.

We use your data because it's needed to provide the service you ask for, for our legitimate interest in preventing fraud, and where the law requires it.

4. Who we share it with

  • Service providers who work for us under contract: hosting (Render, servers in Singapore), file storage, email delivery (Postmark), messaging (WhatsApp messages, through Twilio), and flight data (FlightAware).
  • When you forward a booking from an airline or travel site we haven't seen before, an AI service (Anthropic's Claude) reads it for us to find the flights, travellers and fare.
  • Our payment provider (Stripe), which takes your payment for the company that sells your pass, and that company and its card issuer (Airwallex, for passes sold by GetThere), receive your legal name, date of birth, home address, email address and mobile number when you buy a pass, to take your payment, check your identity (including sanctions screening), and fund and issue the pre-loaded rescue card in your name. The card issuer may ask for an identity document if it can't confirm who you are.
  • The airline, to check your booking is still active before we send a pre-loaded rescue card.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

5. If you bought through a partner

Some partners, such as banks, offer the Flight Delay Pass to their customers. The partner is a separate organisation with its own privacy notice. If you bought through a partner's GetThere page, we share data with it only as follows:

  • To confirm the booking was paid with that partner's card: we send only your email address, the airline, the amount, the currency and the booking date range. The partner answers yes or no and shares no transaction data with us.
  • To show alerts about your pass in the partner's app: we send your email address and the alert's wording and link. You can turn these alerts off in the app.

6. Where it's stored

Our servers are in Singapore. Some of our providers may process data in other countries; when they do, we use contracts that keep it safe.

7. How long we keep it

  • Links in our emails to your pass and pre-loaded rescue card keep working for 12 months after your trip, then stop.
  • We keep booking emails and booking data, including the details you gave at checkout and our copies of the messages we sent you, for 12 months after your last flight, then delete them.
  • We keep offer page visits for 12 months. Then we delete the visit, or, if you forwarded a booking from it, delete the page you came from and its campaign tags, and keep only the link between your booking and the offer.
  • We keep payment and pre-loaded rescue card records for as long as the law requires.

8. Your choices and rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, by emailing privacy@getthere.now. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to object to some uses or to complain to your privacy regulator.

Forward a booking only if you are a traveller on it or have the travellers' agreement to share their details with us.

9. Cookies

Our pages don't use cookies for tracking or advertising.

10. Changes

We'll post any changes to this notice on this page with a new effective date.
