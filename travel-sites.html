How it's different

Not the airline's next free seat. Not a refund weeks later.

Airline rebooking

The airline moves your customer to its own next free seat, often hours later or the next day, after a queue or a wait on the phone. With the pass, the Underwriter puts money for a new flight in their hands within minutes of a 3-hour delay, to spend on your site only.

Claims after the trip

Refunds and compensation arrive weeks later, after forms and receipts, and don't get them there on time. With the pass there's nothing to claim: live flight data triggers the rescue card, and they rebook on the spot.

The Underwriter

The Underwriter collects every pass fee and funds every rescue card. You never fund anything.

The Underwriter is the company offering the Flight Delay Pass to your customers. Your customers pay their pass fees to the Underwriter. From those fees the Underwriter pays for a pre-loaded rescue card for every pass holder whose flight is delayed 3+ hours or cancelled, pays your share, and pays GetThere a platform fee to run the service.

Collects every pass fee

The customer pays the Underwriter, through its card payment provider.

Funds every rescue card

From the fees it collects, the Underwriter pays every pass holder whose flight is delayed 3+ hours or cancelled, as a pre-loaded rescue card. A card provider it chooses issues each rescue card.

Pays your share

Your share of each pass fee is agreed with the Underwriter and paid monthly from the fees it collects.

GetThere is the platform. It runs everything else for the Underwriter: offer pages, pricing, checkout, flight tracking, rescue card delivery and support.

Risk, legal and privacy

Answers for your risk, legal and privacy teams

Regulatory position

The Flight Delay Pass is a policy, issued and underwritten by the Underwriter under its own licence. GetThere is its platform provider. If a flight is delayed 3+ hours or cancelled, the customer gets a single-use card for new flights on your site; it buys new flights only and is never paid out as cash.

Fraud

Before giving a price, GetThere checks that the flight exists, the fare looks right and the traveller has no history of abuse. It checks again before sending any rescue card.

Data

The customer details you pass are used only to fill in the checkout, and are deleted once sales close, 48 hours before departure.

Vendor review

GetThere provides its security, data-handling and business-continuity pack up front. GetThere holds no card data: card details are typed into the payment form of the Underwriter's payment provider, embedded on the page.
