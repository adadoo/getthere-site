The flow

From booking confirmed to rescue card, in four steps

YOUR SITE

1. Booking confirmed

Your server calls POST /quotes with the flights, the fare paid, the passengers and the booker's email.

YOUR SITE

2. Offer on the confirmation page

You show the plans GetThere returned: the fee and the rescue card limit for each, in the booking's currency. Declined bookings come back with a reason, and you simply show nothing.

YOUR SITE

3. Customer picks a plan

Your server calls POST /checkouts with the offer and everything you know about the customer, and redirects them to the checkout_url it gets back.

GETTHERE

4. Checkout, filled in

Name, date of birth, home address and mobile are already there. The customer checks them, confirms their WhatsApp number, agrees to the terms and pays the Underwriter.

What happens next is GetThere's

The pass confirmation by email, flight tracking from 48 hours before departure, and if the flight is delayed 3+ hours or cancelled, the rescue card by WhatsApp and email within minutes. No claim form.

The customer can switch plans

Both plans on the quote open filled in, so a customer who changes their mind at checkout keeps their details.

One pass per booking

Quote the same booking again and you get the same open offers. A booking that already has a pass comes back as already_has_pass.

The API

Two calls, one key

Base URL https://api.getthere.now/partner/v1. Every call takes Authorization: Bearer <key>. Keys come from the GetThere admin: gt_test_… makes test passes with test payments, test rescue cards and simulated delays; gt_live_… works once you're live. Call from your server, never from the browser.

1. Quote the booking

Right after the booking is confirmed. Times are local at each airport. airline is the airline that issued the ticket; without it, the first flight's airline.

2. Open the checkout

When the customer picks a plan. Send whatever you have; every field in customer is optional.

POST /checkouts

  "offer_id": "de01b698-…",
  "customer": {
    "first_name": "Mia", "last_name": "Tan",
    "date_of_birth": "1990-04-02",
    "phone": "+6581234567",
    "address": {"line1": "1 Raffles Place", "line2": "#20-01",
                "city": "Singapore", "postcode": "048616",
                "country": "SG"}
  }
}
200
{
  "checkout_url": "https://yoursite.getthere.now/checkout/jPib…",
  "expires_at": "2026-10-14T20:43:30Z",
  "prefilled": ["city", "country", "date_of_birth", "first_name",
                "last_name", "line1", "line2", "phone", "postcode"],
  "not_prefilled": {}
}

Redirect the customer to checkout_url. A field the checkout wouldn't accept (a name not in English letters, someone under 18, a number not in international format) is left for the customer to type and listed in not_prefilled with the reason. 404: not your offer; 409: the offer has closed or the booking already has a pass.

What we keep. The details you send are used only to fill in the checkout, and are deleted once sales close, 48 hours before departure. What the customer confirms at checkout becomes the rescue card holder's details, as the Privacy Notice says.

Pass fees = flights sold × percent of bookings that buy a pass × 7.5% (the pass fee before tax). The profit pool is 40% of the fees, after rescue card money, payment and card costs and GetThere's platform fee. Pass numbers assume a $500 average booking, and 2 in 100 passes end with a rescue card.

Your share comes from the profit pool

The Underwriter collects every fee, pays the rescue cards, payment costs and GetThere's platform fee, then splits what's left with you as you agree. It's paid monthly.

The price is the same for your customer

Your share comes out of the fee, never on top of it. Sales tax for the country the customer lives in is added at checkout.

Quotes, passes sold, your share, customers rescued and their feedback, with your top routes and airlines.
