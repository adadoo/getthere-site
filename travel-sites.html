How it's different

Not the airline's next free seat. Not a refund weeks later.

Airline rebooking

The airline moves your customer to its next free seat, often hours later or the next day, after a queue. With the pass, they get money for a new flight within minutes of a 3-hour delay, to spend on your site only.

Claims after the trip

Refunds come weeks later, after forms and receipts. With the pass there's nothing to claim: live flight data triggers the rescue card and they rebook on the spot.

Your rewards program

Run your own points? Customers earn and spend them on the pass

EARN

Points on every pass

Give back part of your share as points, on the fee before tax. Credit them from the pass bought event, which carries your member id. A cooling-off refund takes them back.

Example: 4 points a dollar on a $37.50 pass is 150 points, from a share of about $7.50.

BURN

A pass voucher paid with points

Members swap points for a pass voucher on your site. It's applied at checkout, paying all or part (card for the rest). We invoice you monthly only for vouchers used. Checkout shows only your program.

Sales tax is charged on the full fee, and the rescue card is the same whichever way they pay.

No program? Nothing changes. Developers: pass events are in the API guide, and issuing vouchers is on the basics page.

Risk, legal and privacy

Answers for your risk, legal and privacy teams

Regulatory position

A policy issued and underwritten by the Underwriter under its own licence; GetThere is its platform provider. A 3+ hour delay or cancellation gets a single-use card for new flights on your site, never cash.

Fraud

Before pricing, GetThere checks the flight exists, the fare looks right and the traveller has no history of abuse. It checks again before sending a rescue card.

Data

Name and email stay with the quote so the pass reaches the customer. Anything else, like date of birth and address, only fills in checkout and is deleted when sales close, 48 hours before departure.

Vendor review

GetThere shares its security, data-handling and business-continuity pack up front. It holds no card data; card details go into the Underwriter's payment gateway.
