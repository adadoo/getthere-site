Base URL and format

Every call goes to https://api.getthere.now/partner/v1, over HTTPS, with JSON in and out (Content-Type: application/json). Times are ISO 8601 with a time zone. Times we send are in UTC, with microseconds and +00:00, such as 2026-11-20T07:41:05.120345+00:00: parse them with a full ISO 8601 parser. Times you send may end in Z or any offset. Dates are YYYY-MM-DD. Flight times are the local time at that airport, as printed on the ticket. Amounts are numbers in the currency named next to them. Currencies are 3-letter ISO codes and countries are 2-letter ISO codes.

Call us from your server, never from a browser or app. Your key would be visible to anyone.

Keys: test and live

Authorization: Bearer gt_test_…   (test key)
Authorization: Bearer gt_live_…   (live key)
KeyWhat it doesWhy it matters
gt_test_…Sees and makes test data only: test quotes, test passes, test pushes. Works at any time, before and after you go live.You can build and keep testing against production with no risk of selling a real pass or touching a real customer.
gt_live_…Sees and makes real data. Answers 403 until we switch your partner to live.The key, not the address, decides test or live, so the same code runs in both. Swap the key to go live.

The key also tells us who you are, so you only ever see your own data. We show each key once, keep only its last 4 characters, and can revoke and reissue one at any time. Ask for a test key at partner@getthere.now. GET /partner/v1/ping checks a key: 200 {"ok": true, "partner": "<you>", "mode": "test"}.

Errors and what to do about them

Every error is JSON with a detail string. Where a call can be refused for a business reason, the detail starts with a machine-readable reason and a colon, like "amount_mismatch: amount_paid must be 34.08 SGD …". Every business 409 carries one. Branch on the part before the colon. The rest is for your logs and support team.

StatusMeansWhat to do
200, 201Done. 201 means something new was made, and 200 on the same call means you'd already made it.Treat both as success.
401Missing, wrong or revoked key.Check the header. Don't retry.
403A live key before your partner is live.Use your test key until we switch you live.
404Not yours, or not in this mode. A test key can't see a live offer and the other way round.Check you're using the key that made it.
409Refused for a business reason, named before the colon.Don't retry the same call. Each guide lists every reason and what to do.
422A field is missing or malformed. detail names it, like "segments[1].departure.time must be HH:MM, local time at the airport".Fix the request. Don't retry it unchanged.
503Your partner is paused. No quotes or offers are made.Show nothing and carry on with your own flow. Calls about passes already sold still work.
5xx, timeoutSomething went wrong on our side, or the network failed.Retry with backoff (see below). Calls that create something are safe to retry.

Retries and idempotency

  • Reads are always safe to retry.
  • Quotes: quoting the same booking or cart again returns the same open offers rather than new ones.
  • Creating a pass (travel sites selling in-line, in their own checkout) is keyed on your payment_reference, per mode: a test sale and a live sale never share one. Sending it again in the same mode returns the pass already made, with 200 instead of 201. Retry until you get an answer, so a paying customer is never left without their pass. A flight change sent again with the same payment_reference returns the same answer too.
  • Pushes and webhooks we send you each carry a unique id. Use it to ignore a repeat.
  • Back off between retries: 1s, 2s, 4s, 8s, then every minute. Stop on any 4xx.

Test mode

Every partner starts in test mode. A test pass runs the whole journey with nothing real:

  • a test payment page instead of a card payment (GetThere's checkout), or no payment check at all (a travel site's own checkout, in-line);
  • a test pre-loaded rescue card that can't be charged, with a "Simulate a purchase" button on its page;
  • emails whose subject starts with "[Test]", and WhatsApp messages that are recorded rather than sent.

On request we can delay or cancel a test pass's flight, so you see the rescue card a few minutes later instead of on travel day. Test passes stay test passes after you go live, so you can keep testing.

Answer quickly with any 2xx and do the work afterwards. We send events on a sweep that runs every few minutes, and wait 10 seconds for your answer. Anything else, or no answer, is tried again on a later sweep: after n tries, not until 2n − 1 minutes after the event (1, 3, 7, 15, 31 … 511 minutes). That's 10 tries in all, the last about 8.5 hours after the event, then we stop. If your address times out or can't be reached, your other events wait for the next sweep. Events happen only while an address is set for that mode: nothing is kept for an address added later.

Checking a webhook's signature

Every webhook is signed with your webhook secret (whsec_…), which we give you once when your first address is set. GetThere-Signature holds the time we signed it (t) and an HMAC-SHA256 of t, a full stop and the raw body (v1, in hex). Check it on the raw bytes, before parsing the JSON, and reject anything more than 5 minutes old. Without the check, anyone who finds your address could tell you a rescue card was used.

// Node (Express): app.post("/getthere/events", express.raw({type: "application/json"}), handler)
const crypto = require("crypto");
function verified(req, secret) {
  const parts = Object.fromEntries(req.get("GetThere-Signature").split(",").map(p => p.split("=")));
  const expected = crypto.createHmac("sha256", secret).update(`${parts.t}.`).update(req.body).digest("hex");
  const fresh = Math.abs(Date.now() / 1000 - Number(parts.t)) < 300;
  return fresh && crypto.timingSafeEqual(Buffer.from(expected), Buffer.from(parts.v1 || ""));
}
# Python
import hashlib, hmac, time

def verified(header: str, raw_body: bytes, secret: str) -> bool:
    parts = dict(p.split("=", 1) for p in header.split(","))
    expected = hmac.new(secret.encode(), f"{parts['t']}.".encode() + raw_body, hashlib.sha256).hexdigest()
    fresh = abs(time.time() - int(parts["t"])) < 300
    return fresh and hmac.compare_digest(expected, parts.get("v1", ""))

If your secret leaks, ask us for a new one. The old one stops working at once.
