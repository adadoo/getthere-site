The product

Money for a new flight within minutes of the delay

All your app has to send is one push notification when you see a flight booked. The member pays the pass fee to the Underwriter, and GetThere runs the rest for it: the price, checkout, flight tracking, and the rescue card by WhatsApp and email. The push notifications in steps 3 to 5 are optional.

Award bookings

Coverage for the flights your members book with miles

On an award booking, the money paid is usually just the taxes, so cover priced from the money paid would be close to worthless. GetThere values the flights at the miles used × what that programme's miles are worth, plus the money paid, never above what the same seats cost to buy today, and sizes the rescue card from that.

A member stranded on an award flight pays cash for a new seat, so the rescue card matters more to them, not less.

How it's different

Not the airline's next free seat. Not a refund weeks later.

Airline rebooking

The airline moves your member to its own next free seat, often hours later or the next day, after a queue or a wait on the phone. With the pass, the Underwriter puts money for a new flight in her hands within minutes of a 3-hour delay, to spend on any airline.

Claims after the trip

Refunds and compensation arrive weeks later, after forms and receipts, and don't get her there on time. With the pass there's nothing to claim: live flight data triggers the rescue card, and she rebooks on the spot.

Earn and burn

Members earn your miles on the pass, and soon spend them on it

EARN

Miles on every pass

Your share of each pass fee works like a merchant commission: give part of it back as your miles. Members earn on the fee before tax, and you credit them from the pass bought event, which carries your own id for the member. A refund in the cooling-off period takes them back.

Example: 4 miles a dollar on a $37.50 pass is 150 miles, from a share of about $7.50.

BURN · COMING NEXT

A pass voucher paid with miles

Members swap miles for a pass voucher in your app and enter its code at checkout, alone or with a card for the rest. There is nothing to buy up front: each month you settle only the vouchers members used, taken off your share, and you set how many miles each one costs. It gives members a useful, everyday way to spend miles between award flights.

Sales tax is charged on the full fee, and the rescue card is the same whichever way she pays.

Your developers will find how to credit miles in the API guide.

The Underwriter

The Underwriter is paid every pass fee and funds every rescue card. You never fund anything.

The Underwriter is the company offering the Flight Delay Pass to your members. Your members pay their pass fees to the Underwriter. From those fees the Underwriter pays for a pre-loaded rescue card for every pass holder whose flight is delayed 3+ hours or cancelled, pays your share, and pays GetThere a platform fee to run the service.

Is paid every pass fee

The member pays GetThere, the Underwriter's authorized collection agent, through Stripe. GetThere passes the Underwriter its share.

Funds every rescue card

From the fees it collects, the Underwriter pays every pass holder whose flight is delayed 3+ hours or cancelled, as a pre-loaded rescue card. A card provider it chooses issues each rescue card.

Pays your share

Your share of each pass fee is agreed with the Underwriter and paid monthly from the fees it is paid. The miles you give members come out of it.

GetThere is the platform. It runs everything else for the Underwriter: offer pages, pricing, checkout, flight tracking, rescue card delivery and support.

Money and data

You never hold the pass money, and nobody sees payment card data

Collected for the Underwriter

Every pass is paid to GetThere's Stripe account, as the Underwriter's authorized collection agent, and passed on to the Underwriter's own Stripe account. Every rescue card is issued from the Underwriter's own card account.

No payment card data

Payment card details are typed into Stripe's payment page. GetThere never sees them, and neither do you.

Your members' data stays with you

Members forward their own booking email. You share no transaction or tracking data with GetThere.

Your share, from the records

Every payment, every refund of a pass voided in the cooling-off period, and every rescue card purchase is recorded as it happens. Your monthly share is worked out from those records, and so are the miles you credit.

Risk, legal and privacy

Answers for your risk, legal and privacy teams

Regulatory position

The Flight Delay Pass is a policy, issued and underwritten by the Underwriter under its own licence. GetThere is its platform provider. How you're paid for introducing members is set in your agreement with the Underwriter, to fit the rules where you operate. If a flight is delayed 3+ hours or cancelled, the member gets a single-use card for new flights; it buys new flights only and is never paid out as cash.

Your card data terms

If you see bookings through a card network's card-linked programme, check its terms allow you to send your own offer after a tracked charge. GetThere never receives that data.

Fraud

Before giving a price, GetThere checks that the flight exists, the fare or miles look right, the booking is real and the traveller has no history of abuse. It checks again before sending any rescue card.

Vendor review

GetThere provides its security, data-handling and business-continuity pack up front. GetThere holds no card data: card details are typed into Stripe's payment page.
