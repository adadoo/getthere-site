Award bookings

Coverage for flights booked with miles

On award bookings the cash paid is usually just taxes. So GetThere values the flights at miles used × value per mile, plus the cash paid, capped at today's cash price for the seats, and sizes the rescue card from that.

A member stranded on an award flight pays cash for a new seat, so the rescue card matters more, not less.

How it's different

Not the airline's next free seat. Not a refund weeks later.

Airline rebooking

The airline moves her to its next free seat, often hours or a day later, after a queue. With the pass, she has money for a flight on any airline within minutes of a 3-hour delay.

Claims after the trip

Refunds take weeks of forms and don't get her there on time. With the pass there's no claim: live flight data triggers the rescue card and she rebooks on the spot.

Earn and burn

Members earn your miles on the pass, and spend them on it

EARN

Miles on every pass

Your share works like a merchant commission: give part back as miles, earned on the fee before tax. Credit them from the pass bought event, which carries your member id. A cooling-off refund takes them back.

Example: 4 miles a dollar on a $37.50 pass is 150 miles, from a share of about $7.50.

BURN

A pass voucher paid with miles

Members swap miles on your redemption page for a fixed-value voucher, applied at checkout, and pay the rest by card. You set the miles price. Nothing up front: we invoice you monthly for vouchers used. An everyday way to spend miles between award flights.

Sales tax is charged on the full fee, and the rescue card is the same however she pays.

The Underwriter

The Underwriter is paid every pass fee and funds every rescue card. You never fund anything.

From the fees it collects, the Underwriter funds a pre-loaded rescue card for every pass holder whose flight is delayed 3+ hours or cancelled, pays your share, and pays GetThere a platform fee.

STRONGEST

A click-through to an airline or travel site

NEAR REAL TIME

Risk, legal and privacy

Answers for your risk, legal and privacy teams

Regulatory position

The pass is a policy issued and underwritten by the Underwriter under its own licence; GetThere is its platform. Your agreement sets how you're paid for introducing members, to fit local rules. The single-use rescue card buys new flights only, never cash.

Your card data terms

Card-linked program terms are yours to check (see above). GetThere never receives that data.

Fraud

Before pricing, GetThere checks that the flight and booking are real, the fare or miles look right and the traveller has no history of abuse. It checks again before any rescue card.

Vendor review

GetThere shares its security, data-handling and business-continuity pack up front. It holds no card data.
