How it's different

Not the airline's next free seat. Not a refund weeks later.

Airline rebooking

The airline moves your cardholder to its own next free seat, often hours later or the next day, after a queue or a wait on the phone. GetThere puts money for a new flight in her hands within minutes of a 3-hour delay, to spend on any airline.

Claims after the trip

Refunds and compensation arrive weeks later, after forms and receipts, and don't get her there on time. With GetThere there's nothing to claim: live flight data triggers the rescue card, and she rebooks on the spot.

The Underwriter

The Underwriter collects every pass fee and funds every rescue card. You never fund anything.

The Underwriter is the company offering the Flight Delay Pass to your cardholders. Your cardholders pay their pass fees to the Underwriter. From those fees the Underwriter pays for a pre-loaded rescue card for every pass holder whose flight is delayed 3+ hours or cancelled, pays your share, and pays GetThere a platform fee to run the service.

Collects every pass fee

The cardholder pays the Underwriter, through its card payment provider.

Funds every rescue card

From the fees it collects, the Underwriter pays every pass holder whose flight is delayed 3+ hours or cancelled, as a pre-loaded rescue card. A card provider it chooses issues each card.

Pays your share

Your share of each pass fee is agreed with the Underwriter and paid monthly from the fees it collects.

GetThere is the platform. It runs everything else for the Underwriter: offer pages, pricing, checkout, flight tracking, rescue card delivery and support.

Risk, legal and privacy

Answers for your risk, legal and privacy teams

Regulatory position

The Flight Delay Pass is an insurance product, sold and underwritten by the Underwriter under its own licence. GetThere is its platform provider. If a flight is delayed 3+ hours or cancelled, the cardholder gets a single-use card for new flights; it buys new flights only and is never paid out as cash.

Fraud

Before giving a price, GetThere checks that the flight exists, the fare looks right, the booking is real and the traveller has no history of abuse. It checks again before sending any rescue card.

Data

Cardholders forward their own booking email. You share no transaction data; the optional purchase check returns only yes or no.

Vendor review

GetThere provides its security, data-handling and business-continuity pack up front. GetThere holds no card data: card details are typed into the payment form of the Underwriter's payment provider, embedded on the page.
