The product

Card benefits and rewards

Let cardholders burn points on the pass

Cardholders can swap points for a pass, earn points when they pay for one with your card, or get one free as a card benefit. It's a reason to reach for your card when they book.

BURN

A pass paid with points

List the pass on your redemption page beside your other redemption partners. Cardholders swap points there for a pass voucher worth a fixed amount, and find it already applied at checkout, with a card for the rest. Nothing to buy up front: each month we invoice you only for the vouchers used.

Checkout only ever shows your own program. Sales tax is charged on the full fee, and the rescue card is the same whichever way they pay.

EARN

Points on every pass

Paid with your card, the pass already earns your usual card rewards. You can add bonus points from your share of the fee. Cardholders earn on the fee before tax, and you credit them from the pass bought event, which carries your own id for the cardholder.

Example: 4 points a dollar on a $37.50 pass is 150 points, from a share of about $7.50.

CARD BENEFIT

A free pass for premium cardholders

Give your top-tier cardholders pass vouchers as a card benefit, paid from your benefits budget. They enter the code at checkout, and you settle each month only for the vouchers they used.

You choose who gets them and how many: every trip, a few a year, or as a welcome offer.

Your developers will find how to credit points in the API guide, and how your redemption page works in the API guide.

How it's different

Not the airline's next free seat. Not a refund weeks later.

Airline rebooking

The airline moves your cardholder to its own next free seat, often hours later or the next day, after a queue or a wait on the phone. With the pass, the Underwriter puts money for a new flight in her hands within minutes of a 3-hour delay, to spend on any airline.

Claims after the trip

Refunds and compensation arrive weeks later, after forms and receipts, and don't get her there on time. With the pass there's nothing to claim: live flight data triggers the rescue card, and she rebooks on the spot.

The Underwriter

The Underwriter is paid every pass fee and funds every rescue card. You never fund anything.

The Underwriter is the company offering the Flight Delay Pass to your cardholders. Your cardholders pay their pass fees to the Underwriter. From those fees the Underwriter pays for a pre-loaded rescue card for every pass holder whose flight is delayed 3+ hours or cancelled, pays your share, and pays GetThere a platform fee to run the service.

Is paid every pass fee

The cardholder pays straight into the Underwriter's own payment gateway account, on a checkout GetThere runs for it. The Underwriter holds all the money; GetThere never does.

Funds every rescue card

From the fees it collects, the Underwriter pays every pass holder whose flight is delayed 3+ hours or cancelled, as a pre-loaded rescue card. A card provider it chooses issues each rescue card.

Pays your share

Your share of each pass fee is agreed with the Underwriter and paid monthly from the fees it is paid.

GetThere is the platform. It runs everything else for the Underwriter: pricing, checkout, flight tracking, rescue card delivery and support.

Money and data

Only the Underwriter holds the pass money, and nobody sees payment card data

Held by the Underwriter

Every pass is paid straight into the Underwriter's own payment gateway account, and every rescue card is issued and funded from its own card issuer account. The Underwriter holds all the money and pays your share and GetThere's platform fee; GetThere only orchestrates each pass on its behalf.

No payment card data

Payment card details are typed into the payment page of the Underwriter's payment gateway. GetThere never sees them, and neither do you.

Your cardholders' data stays with you

Cardholders forward their own booking email. You share no transaction data, and the optional purchase check returns only yes or no.

Your share, from the records

Every payment, every refund of a pass voided in the cooling-off period, and every rescue card purchase is recorded as it happens. Your monthly share is worked out from those records, with no manual invoicing.

Sales tax shown before they pay. The Underwriter sets the sales tax for each country it sells in. Checkout adds it for the buyer's country of residence and shows it before they pay, and the Underwriter files it.

Risk, legal and privacy

Answers for your risk, legal and privacy teams

Regulatory position

The Flight Delay Pass is a policy, issued and underwritten by the Underwriter under its own licence. GetThere is its platform provider. If a flight is delayed 3+ hours or cancelled, the cardholder gets a single-use card for new flights; it buys new flights only and is never paid out as cash.

Fraud

Before giving a price, GetThere checks that the flight exists, the fare looks right, the booking is real and the traveller has no history of abuse. It checks again before sending any rescue card.

Data

Cardholders forward their own booking email. You share no transaction data; the optional purchase check returns only yes or no.

Vendor review

GetThere provides its security, data-handling and business-continuity pack up front. GetThere holds no card data: card details are typed into the payment page of the Underwriter's payment gateway.
