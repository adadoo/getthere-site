Risk, legal and privacy

Answers for your risk, legal and privacy teams

Regulatory position

A paid rebooking service. The pre-loaded rescue card buys new flights only and is never paid out as cash. GetThere provides a legal opinion before launch.

Fraud

Before quoting, GetThere checks the flight exists, the fare is believable, the booking is real and the traveller's history. It checks again before issuing any pre-loaded rescue card.

Data

Cardholders forward their own booking email. You share no transaction data; the optional purchase check returns only yes or no.

Vendor review

GetThere provides its security, data-handling and business-continuity pack up front, including card-data (PCI) compliance.
