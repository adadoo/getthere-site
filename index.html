Questions

Good to know

What exactly do I get?

Help getting to where you're going. If a flight on your booking is delayed 3+ hours or cancelled, we send you a single-use pre-loaded rescue card to book a new flight. It works for 24 hours, for one purchase, at airlines and travel agencies only. It buys new flights only and is never paid out as cash. There's nothing to fill in and no receipts to send.

Your original ticket stays yours.

Ask the airline for any refund or compensation it owes you, as you normally would. The rescue card comes on top of that.

Spend less than the rescue card holds?

The rescue card is for one purchase and is never paid out as cash. Rescue card money is spent first. Any of your top-up money left over after your purchase, or when the rescue card expires, is refunded to the card you paid with. Rescue card money you don't spend isn't paid out.

How much does it cost?

It depends on how likely your flight is to be disrupted on your travel date, and on your airfare. Forward your booking to see your price; there's no obligation to buy.

When do I need to buy it?

At least 48 hours before your first flight. Your price stays valid for 12 hours. After that, forward the booking again for a new price.

What does one pass include?

Every flight and every traveller on the booking you forward. If any of those flights is delayed 3+ hours or cancelled, we send the rescue card.

How do I get the rescue card?

We send you a link to your rescue card on WhatsApp and by email as soon as the delay is confirmed. Use it with any airline or travel agency, and book everything you need for the rest of the trip in that one purchase. If the new flight costs more than the rescue card holds, you can pay the difference yourself.

Which bookings can I forward?

Confirmation or e-ticket emails for flights booked with any airline or travel agency. You need to live in a country where we can issue your pre-loaded rescue card. If we can't price a booking, we'll email you to say why.

What happens to my email?

We read the booking details we need to price and watch your flights: flight numbers, dates, travellers and airfare. Our Privacy Notice has the details.
