Questions

Good to know

How is this different from travel insurance?

Travel insurance pays you back later, after a claim. The Flight Delay Pass gets you moving straight away: if your flight is 3 or more hours late or cancelled, you get a pre-loaded rescue card to book a new flight. The rescue card buys new flights only and is never paid out as cash. There's nothing to claim and no receipts to send.

How much does it cost?

It depends on how likely your flight is to be disrupted on your travel date, and on your fare. Forward your booking to see your price; there's no obligation to buy.

When do I need to buy it?

At least 48 hours before your first flight. Your price stays valid for 24 hours. After that, forward the booking again for a new price.

What does one pass include?

Every flight and every traveller on the booking you forward. If any of those flights is 3 or more hours late or cancelled, we send the rescue card.

How do I get the rescue card, and where can I use it?

We email you a link to your rescue card as soon as the delay is confirmed. Use it within 24 hours at any airline or travel agency, online or by phone. It buys new flights only and can't be paid out as cash. If the new flight costs more than the card holds, you can pay the difference yourself.

Which bookings can I forward?

Confirmation or e-ticket emails from Air India, AirAsia, Emirates and 3 more, or any other airline or travel site (we learn new ones as they're forwarded), for most bookings worth about US$150 to US$12,000 (or the same in your currency). If we can't price a booking, we'll email you to say why.

What happens to my email?

We read the booking details we need to price and watch your flights: flight numbers, dates, travellers and fare. Our Privacy Notice has the details.
