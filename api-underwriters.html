For underwriters

Integrations for underwriters

Version 1 · Draft · Written for the team that sets up your accounts. The business side is on the underwriter page.

The short version. You bring your own payment gateway and your own rescue card issuer. Every pass sold under your policy is paid into your gateway account, and every rescue card is issued and funded on your issuer account. GetThere builds and runs the integration with each vendor, so you only open the accounts, enter the keys in the GetThere admin and point each vendor's webhooks at the addresses it shows you.

1. How it fits together

You bring

  • A payment gateway account, in your name. The traveller pays you on it, and its refunds and disputes are yours.
  • A card issuer account, in your name. Each rescue card is issued and funded on it.
  • Sandbox and live keys for each, entered in the GetThere admin.
  • The countries you serve, with your sales tax in each, and which of your vendors each one uses.

GetThere provides

  • The integration with each vendor: checkout on your gateway, refunds and dispute evidence, cardholders, cards, top-ups and real-time card approvals on your issuer.
  • A webhook address for each of your accounts, checked against your own signing secret.
  • A check of your keys before your policy is sold, in test mode and again for real passes.
  • A sandbox to start on while your own test accounts are being set up.

Each country you serve picks one payment gateway and one card issuer from the vendors that work there. Your partners only distribute your policy: a buyer living in that country pays on your gateway and gets a rescue card from your issuer, whichever partner they bought through. A change applies to the next payment and the next card; payments and cards already made stay on the account they were made on, for refunds, top-ups and changes.

3. Stripe (payments)

You enter · for test and live separately, on your Payments and cards page in the admin

FieldWhat it isWhy
Secret key requiredYour sk_test_… or sk_live_… key, or a restricted rk_… key with the permissions below.Every call we make on your account uses it.
Publishable keyYour pk_test_… or pk_live_… key.Needed to sell in-line: a travel site's checkout uses it to confirm the pass's payment on your account. Without it, real passes can't be sold in-line.
Webhook secret requiredThe whsec_… signing secret of the endpoint you add for GetThere.We only act on events signed with it.

A restricted key needs write access to Checkout Sessions, PaymentIntents, Refunds and Disputes, and read access to Charges and Balance.

Passes sold in-line. When a travel site sells the pass in its own checkout, the pass is paid for apart from the flights, on your Stripe account: GetThere makes a PaymentIntent for the pass's price, with manual capture, and the site's checkout confirms it with your publishable key. GetThere asks Stripe about the payment when the site creates the pass, takes it and issues the pass, or cancels it if the pass can't be issued. Refunds in the cooling-off period go back to the customer's card from your account. Nothing is owed between you and the site for these passes.

The webhook. In Stripe, add an endpoint at the payments address your admin page shows, which looks like https://my.getthere.now/webhooks/underwriters/<your id>/payments/stripe, and send it these events:

EventWhat we do
checkout.session.completed
checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded		Issue the pass, or add the top-up to the rescue card.
checkout.session.async_payment_failed
checkout.session.expired		Mark the payment failed, so the buyer can try again.
charge.dispute.created
charge.dispute.updated
charge.dispute.closed		Record the dispute, end the pass if no rescue card came from it yet, and send Stripe the evidence: the terms the buyer accepted, the disrupted flight, and the card and what was spent on it.

The buyer's card statement shows your Stripe account's name. Stripe's fees are charged on your account, as Stripe prices it.

4. Airwallex Issuing (rescue cards)

You enter · for test and live separately, on your Payments and cards page in the admin

FieldWhat it isWhy
Client ID requiredYour Airwallex API client ID.Signs in to your account.
API key requiredThe API key that goes with it, with Issuing enabled.The same.
Card authorisation webhook secret set itThe secret of the remote authorisation endpoint you add for GetThere.We only answer approval requests signed with it, so without it no purchase on a rescue card is approved.

Remote authorisation. In Airwallex, turn on remote authorisation for the card program and point it at the card authorisations address your admin page shows, which looks like https://my.getthere.now/webhooks/underwriters/<your id>/cards/authorization. We approve a purchase only while it fits the rescue card: an airline or travel agency charge, within what is on the card, before the card expires. The first approved purchase closes the card.

The card program. Rescue cards are consumer cards in the traveller's name. Each buyer becomes a cardholder on your account when they pay, so the card can be issued the moment the delay is confirmed. The card number is only ever shown in Airwallex's own frame and never reaches GetThere.

5. Test mode

Test passes use your sandbox keys, and real passes your live keys. Nothing real moves on a test pass.

  • No sandbox payment keys yet? Test passes are paid on GetThere's own Stripe sandbox, with Stripe's test card, so your team can try the whole journey on day one. A travel site testing in-line gets a stub payment it marks paid or failed itself.
  • No sandbox card keys yet? Test rescue cards are made by GetThere, with a button to simulate a purchase.
  • Real passes never fall back. A country sells real passes only once GetThere has activated your own live keys for both its vendors.
  • Sandbox keys count once activated. Until GetThere activates them, test payments stay on GetThere's sandbox.

6. Going live, country by country

  • Your live Stripe and Airwallex Issuing keys are entered, and both webhook endpoints are added with their secrets.
  • Each country you serve is ticked, then its vendors and sales tax are picked: vendors are picked only for a country you serve.
  • GetThere activates each set of keys, one vendor and mode at a time. Activating makes a signed-in call to the vendor with them and says what doesn't work yet. Changing the keys means activating them again.
  • Your partners pick you for your countries. Each one sells real passes there from then on.

7. How your keys are kept

Keys are stored encrypted, test and live apart, and are never shown again: the admin shows only their last four characters. To change one, enter just that field. Keys that a country on sale uses can't be removed until its partners pick another underwriter there. Refunds go back through the account the payment was taken on, so keep those keys in while refunds may still come.

8. A vendor that isn't listed

Each gateway and each issuer is a separate integration behind the same interface, so adding one doesn't change how the others work. If you use a different gateway or issuer in a country, tell us which, and we'll scope the integration with you.
