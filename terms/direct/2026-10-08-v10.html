Flight Delay Pass terms

The short version. If a flight on your booking is 3 hours or more late, or cancelled, we send you a virtual card to book a new flight on any airline. The card buys new flights only and is never paid out as cash. Buy at least 48 hours before your first flight.

1. Who we are

These terms are an agreement between you and GetThere ("we", "us"), which runs the Flight Delay Pass as GetThere. You can reach us at help@my.getthere.now.

2. What the Flight Delay Pass is

The Flight Delay Pass is a paid rebooking service. If a covered flight is badly delayed or cancelled, we give you a single-use virtual payment card, funded by us, to buy a new flight. It is not an insurance policy, and it does not replace any rights you have against the airline, such as refunds, care or compensation under passenger-rights laws.

The card buys new flights only and is never paid out as cash. Any amount you don't spend is not paid to you.

3. Getting a price

  • Forward the booking confirmation or e-ticket email your airline sent you to quote@my.getthere.now. We read bookings from the airlines listed on our website.
  • We reply by email with a price for each plan. A price holds for 24 hours, or until sales close if that's sooner. After that, forward the booking again for a new price.
  • Sales close 48 hours before the first flight on the booking.
  • We can decline to offer a pass. We do this when the booking is outside the values below; the fare is more than 1.5 times the usual highest fare for those flights; we can't find the flights in the airline's schedule or confirm the booking; disruption on the travel date is already very likely; a traveller on the booking has received 2 or more cards in the last 12 months; or you have bought passes for more than 3 bookings in the last 12 months that you're not travelling on. 4. Buying a pass

You choose one plan:

  • Basic: a card for up to 1.5 times the fare you paid.
  • Plus: a card for up to 2 times the fare you paid.

"The fare you paid" is the total shown on your booking for its flights and taxes, in the booking's currency. One pass covers every flight and every traveller on the booking you forwarded. You pay by card at checkout. We ask for a mobile number at checkout so we can send your card there. The information you give us, and the booking you forward, must be real and accurate.

5. When you get a card

  • We start watching each covered flight 24 hours before its scheduled departure.
  • You get a card if a covered flight departs 3 hours or more after its scheduled time, or is cancelled by the airline. "Scheduled time" is the airline's time for the flight when we start watching it.
  • If the airline changes the time of a flight before we start watching it, your pass moves to the new time. We'll email you. A schedule change like this is not a delay.
  • One pass gives one card, for the first covered flight that is delayed or cancelled. The card is sent to the person who bought the pass.
  • Before we issue a card, we may check with the airline that the booking is still active. If the airline tells us it was cancelled or never existed, no card is issued and the pass ends.
  • Once a card is issued, it stands, even if the airline later shortens the delay.

You don't get a card for a delay shorter than 3 hours, a flight you miss or don't take, a booking you cancel or change yourself, or a flight that isn't on the booking you forwarded.

6. Using the card

  • The card holds up to your plan's multiple of the fare you paid. We tell you the amount when we send it.
  • It works for 24 hours from when we send it, at airlines and travel agencies only.
  • It works for one purchase, then closes. Book everything you need for the rest of the trip, such as connections or a return, in that one purchase.
  • If the new flight costs more than the card holds, you can add your own money to the card. Anything you add and don't spend is refunded to you when the card closes.
  • Any amount left on the card when it closes or expires is not refunded or paid out.
  • Airlines often cancel the rest of a booking when you don't take a flight on it. When we send your card we'll tell you if later flights on your booking are at risk, so you can rebook them too.

7. How we contact you

We send your price, your pass, alerts and your card by email to the address you forwarded your booking from; by WhatsApp, or text message if WhatsApp isn't available, to the mobile number you give us at checkout.

When each covered flight takes off on time, we'll email you to say your pass for that flight is complete.

8. Changes and refunds

  • You can cancel your pass for a full refund until we start watching your first flight. Email help@my.getthere.now.
  • If you are charged twice for the same booking, we refund the second payment.
  • If you change or cancel your booking with the airline, tell us. The pass doesn't move to a new booking.

9. Fair use

If we reasonably believe a booking, a claim or a card purchase is false or fraudulent, we can refuse a pass, end it without a card, stop a card, and recover any amount wrongly paid. We may also refuse future passes.

10. Our responsibility to you

We are responsible for providing the service as described in these terms. We are not responsible for what airlines or travel agencies do, including whether seats are available or what they charge. To the extent the law allows, our total liability to you for a pass is limited to the fee you paid for it plus the card amount it promised. Nothing in these terms limits rights you have under consumer law that can't be limited.

11. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms. A pass you've already bought stays under the terms in force when you bought it.

12. Law

These terms are governed by the laws of Singapore. Before going to court, please contact us at help@my.getthere.now so we can try to put things right.
