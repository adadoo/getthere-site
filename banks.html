What you get

Your brand on every touchpoint

Your name, logo, colours and font on the quote email, checkout, pass and card pages, under your own address like yourbank.getthere.now.

Your plans and your share

Name your plans and pick card sizes from 1× to 3× the fare. Your commission from the agreement is applied to every pass sold.

Bookings proven by your card

Purchase match lets your systems confirm a cardholder paid the airline with your card. GetThere sends only what it needs to match; no transaction data comes back.

Alerts in your app

Show "pass bought", "card ready" and "card used" as pushes in your own app. The card itself always goes to the traveller by email.

No claims to handle

GetThere watches every flight, issues the card when it's late, and answers your cardholders' questions.

Works for any airline trip

The card works on any airline or travel agency. Bookings from Singapore Airlines and Virgin Australia today, with more airlines added for every channel at once.

Getting started

From signed agreement to live

Steps marked You are yours; steps marked GetThere happen on our side once the step before is done.

  1. Agree the commercials GetThere

    Done offline when you sign: your commission, which card sizes you can offer, and your launch markets.

  2. Set up your channel You

    Use the channel setup form below: your handle, look, plans, cover and APIs. You see a live preview as you go, then send it to us.

  3. Your address goes live GetThere

    We create <handle>.getthere.now: the forwarding address quote@<handle>.getthere.now, the sending domain, your pages and HTTPS. It finishes once DNS has spread, usually within a few hours.

  4. Receive your keys GetThere

    Two secrets, shared over a secure channel: a partner key you send when calling GetThere, and a bank token GetThere sends when calling you. Until then, the sandbox key stub-<handle> works.

  5. Connect purchase match You · recommended

    One endpoint that answers yes or no: did this cardholder pay this airline about this much? See the API.

  6. Connect app pushes You · optional

    One endpoint that accepts a message for your app, plus delivery receipts back to us.

  7. Test end to end You

    Forward a real Singapore Airlines or Virgin Australia booking to quote@<handle>.getthere.now. You get a quote in your brand, a test checkout, and a pass. Ask us to simulate a delay and you'll see the card arrive.

  8. Launch to cardholders You

    Call GET /partner/v1/offer for your offer page and forwarding address, and put them where cardholders see them: a "Just booked a flight?" push after an airline purchase, your app's travel section, or your statements.

API

Integration reference

JSON over HTTPS. Every call carries a bearer token. Your partner key decides your channel, so you only ever see your own data.

Calls you make to GetThere

Base URL https://<handle>.getthere.now/partner/v1, header Authorization: Bearer <partner key>.

CallWhat it does
GET /pingChecks your key. Returns your channel and whether it's in stub or live mode.
GET /offerYour offer page and forwarding address, for a push or button in your app.
POST /push-receiptsTells us whether pushes reached your cardholders. opted_out stops further pushes to that person.
GET /partner/v1/offer
→ 200 {"offer_url": "https://yourbank.getthere.now/offer",
       "forward_to": "quote@yourbank.getthere.now",
       "product_name": "GetThere"}

POST /partner/v1/push-receipts
{"receipts": [{"push_id": "9b2e…", "status": "delivered",
               "at": "2026-11-20T07:41:05Z"}]}
→ 200 {"results": [{"push_id": "9b2e…", "result": "ok"}]}

Calls GetThere makes to you

Header Authorization: Bearer <bank token>; mutual TLS on top if you ask for it. We wait up to 5 seconds; a timeout or error is treated as "unknown", never as "no".

Purchase match · recommended

Did this cardholder pay this airline about this amount since this date? A charge within 1% of the fare is a match. Answer only yes or no; someone you can't find by email is false.

POST {purchase match base URL}/v1/getthere/purchase-match
{"email": "mia@example.com", "airline": "SQ",
 "amount": "1550.00", "currency": "SGD",
 "booked_after": "2025-10-09T00:00:00Z"}
→ 200 {"match": true}

App push · optional

A message to show in your app. push_id is also the Idempotency-Key header, so a retry never shows twice. kind is pass_purchased, card_issued or card_used.

POST {app push URL}
Idempotency-Key: 9b2e…
{"push_id": "9b2e…", "customer": {"email": "mia@example.com"},
 "kind": "card_issued", "title": "Your card is ready",
 "body": "GetThere has sent it to you by email. Use it for a new flight on any airline.",
 "link": "https://yourbank.getthere.now/pass/…"}
→ 202 {"accepted": true, "reference": "your-id-123"}

Sandbox

Until your live keys are issued, use stub-<handle> as your partner key. Purchase match and pushes are simulated on our side, so you can try the whole journey before your endpoints exist.
